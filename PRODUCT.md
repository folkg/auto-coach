# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS

## Users

Returning Fantasy AutoCoach users arriving for a new season. They may have used the service in a previous season. They need to understand the service has stopped and why. No action is required from them.

## Product Purpose

Fantasy AutoCoach automated Yahoo Fantasy lineup management. The service is shut down because Yahoo does not allow the required write access on its Fantasy Sports API. Success means a visitor understands within seconds that the service has stopped and why, without confusion and without needing to act.

## Positioning

An honest shutdown notice for a single-purpose automation tool blocked by upstream API access policy.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive at the start of a new fantasy season expecting the coach to work. The Yahoo Fantasy Sports API does not currently permit the write access this product requires. Until that changes, the site cannot operate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static, concise shutdown notice.
- No login, no actions, no next steps for the user.
- Tone is final about the shutdown; do not imply a return is likely.
- Keep it simple and visually refined.
- Keep the Fantasy AutoCoach name, trophy mark, and Roby artwork.
- No contact method, timeline, or data-deletion details.

## Brand Commitments

Fantasy AutoCoach name, trophy mark (`decommission-page/assets/auto-coach-mark.png`), and Roby artwork (`decommission-page/assets/roby-hockey.png`).

## Evidence on Hand

Existing client visual implementation in `client/src`, including Roboto typography and deep-purple/amber Material theme. Shutdown page assets are copied under `decommission-page/assets/`. No testimonials, pricing, or metrics to include.

## Product Principles

- Be immediately clear that the service has stopped.
- Explain the Yahoo write-access cause in one plain sentence.
- Ask nothing of the visitor.
- Stay final without sounding bitter or technical.
